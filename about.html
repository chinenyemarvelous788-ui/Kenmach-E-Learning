Our Mission

Make world-class learning genuinely accessible.

Africa has no shortage of talent — it has a shortage of access. Access to mentors who've actually done the work. Access to affordable, practical courses that respect your time.

We built Chigos to close that gap. Every course teaches something you can use this week — not something you'll "someday" need. Every instructor is vetted, verified, and still working in their field.

Our Journey

From a small idea in Lagos to a growing movement.

2021

The idea

Two engineers frustrated by outdated online courses decided to build something better — a platform where practitioners teach what they actually do for a living.

2022

First 10 instructors

We onboarded our first 10 instructors — senior engineers, designers, and data scientists from companies like Paystack, Flutterwave, and Google.

2023

1,000 learners

Chigos crossed 1,000 enrolled learners. Our first cohort of students landed jobs at companies they'd only dreamed of.

2024

Expanded into 21 categories

We grew beyond tech — adding music, cooking, health, languages, and more. Learning became broader than career skills.

2025

12,400+ strong

Today, over 12,000 learners across Africa and beyond call Chigos home. And we're just getting started.
