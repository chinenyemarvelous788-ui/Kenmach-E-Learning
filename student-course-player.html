User research fundamentals

22 min video 3 resources 4 questions

About this lesson

In this lesson we cover the fundamentals of user research — how to plan interviews, conduct them without biasing the participant, and synthesize findings into actionable insights.

By the end of this lesson you should be able to:

  • Write a research plan with clear objectives
  • Recruit 5–8 representative participants
  • Conduct interviews using the "Mom Test" framework
  • Synthesize findings into themes and pain points

Great question! 5–8 is usually enough for qualitative interviews if your audience is fairly homogeneous. If you're hearing the same 3 themes repeatedly, you've hit saturation. For B2B or niche audiences, you may need 10–12.
