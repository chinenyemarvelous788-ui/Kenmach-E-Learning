30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to learn with absolute confidence. If you enroll in any paid course on Chigos and find that it does not meet your expectations, you are entitled to a full refund within 30 days of purchase.

Refund Eligibility Conditions

To prevent abuse while ensuring fair protections for our instructors, refund requests must meet the following criteria:

  • Time Window: Submitted within 30 calendar days of the original purchase date.
  • Consumption Threshold: Less than 50% of the course lectures have been watched.
  • Certificate Status: The course certificate of completion has not been generated or downloaded.
  • Integrity Check: Account is in good standing with no prior fraudulent chargebacks.

How to Request a Refund

  1. Go to your Contact Us form or email support@chigos.com.
  2. Include your account email address and course order ID (found on your payment receipt).
  3. Our billing team will review and process your refund within 24 to 48 hours. Funds return to your original payment method (card or bank account) within 3 to 5 business days depending on your bank.
