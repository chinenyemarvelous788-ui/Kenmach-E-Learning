Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what we hear most

Complete all lessons in the course, then pass the final exam with a score of 70% or higher. Your certificate is issued automatically and appears on your Certificates page within minutes. You can download it as a PDF or share a public verification link.

Yes. You get two attempts per exam. If you don't pass on the second attempt, there's a 24-hour cooldown before you can try again. Review the lessons you struggled with; your exam report shows which questions you missed.

You can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if you've watched less than 25% of the course. Go to Purchases, find the transaction, and select "Request refund". Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

Downloads are available on the Chigos mobile app for iOS and Android. Tap the download icon next to a lesson to save it for offline viewing. Downloads expire after 30 days and can be renewed while you're enrolled in the course.

Try refreshing the page and clearing your browser cache. Make sure you're using the latest version of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. Restricted networks can block video streaming; try another network to check.

Go to Settings → Account to update your email, or Settings → Security to change your password. If you've forgotten your password, sign out and choose "Forgot password" on the login screen. Email changes require verification from your new address.

Enrollments are personal and can't be transferred. You can gift a course: find it in Explore and choose "Gift this course" to purchase it for someone else. They'll receive an email invitation to redeem it.
