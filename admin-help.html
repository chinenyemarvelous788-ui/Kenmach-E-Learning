Commission Architecture

Chigos deducts a flat ₦100 fee per checkout. Teachers receive (Course Price - ₦100) credited directly to their balance.

Teacher Registration

Instructors pay a one-time ₦2,000 onboarding charge to activate channel publishing privileges on Chigos.

Client-Side Persistence

All records run via ChigosStorage inside your browser's persistent storage. Works offline without external backends.

Account Shortcuts

Frequently Asked Questions

Common operational questions regarding courses, users, and platform payments.

Q: How are course payouts computed for instructors?

Whenever a student purchases a course on Chigos, the system automatically registers the transaction under chigos.purchases. The student pays the listed price, the platform retains ₦100, and the remainder is added to the instructor's balance. When the admin clicks "Pay Out Now" on the Payments page, the instructor's balance resets to zero and an audit entry is recorded.

Q: What happens when an administrator removes a course?

Removing a course via the Courses tab removes it from the public catalog search results. However, students who previously bought the course retain lifetime access in their purchased dashboard library.
