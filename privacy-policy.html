01

Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Chigos Learning Ltd. ("Chigos", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data obtained from learners, instructors, corporate partners, and visitors ("Users", "you") across chigoselearning.com, mobile applications, APIs, and associated web services.

We are fully committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), and international privacy benchmarks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Plain English Summary

We only ask for information that lets you learn, buy courses, stream videos, earn certificates, or get paid as a teacher. We treat your personal information with the highest security and never sell your data.

02

Information We Collect

We collect personal information depending on your interaction with the platform:

Category Data Points Collected Primary Purpose
Account & Profile Full name, email address, salted password hash, optional display picture, bio, and student/instructor role. Account creation, authentication, role-based dashboard access, and communications.
Learning Progress Course enrollments, video watch timestamps, quiz scores, homework submissions, and completion dates. Tracking course completion, resume playback, issuing verified certificates, and personalized recommendations.
Billing & Orders Order ID, course purchased, currency, amount paid, payment receipt reference, and transaction timestamp. Order fulfillment, tax compliance, invoices, and accounting.
Teacher Identity (KYC) Government ID number, Bank Verification Number (BVN) / National Identification Number (NIN) hash, and Nigerian bank account number. Verification of instructor eligibility, regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, and channel earnings payouts.
Device & Analytics IP address, browser type, operating system version, country/region, referral source, and error crash logs. Service uptime monitoring, security diagnostics, fraud prevention, and speed optimization.
03

How We Use Your Data

We process your personal information only when we have a legitimate legal basis, such as fulfilling our contract with you, complying with statutory legal obligations, or with your explicit consent:

  • Deliver Core Educational Services: Provide uninterrupted video streaming, course navigation, quizzes, instructor Q&A sessions, and resource downloads.
  • Issue Cryptographically Verifiable Certificates: Generate tamper-proof digital certificates stamped with unique verification IDs, student name, and completion date.
  • Account & Security Communications: Send order receipts, password resets, multi-factor authentication tokens, and urgent service updates.
  • Community Protection: Monitor against account takeover, multiple concurrent device misuse, video piracy, scraping, and abusive forum behavior.
  • Product Enhancements: Analyze anonymized video buffering rates and completion statistics to make courses easier and faster to stream over mobile networks.
04

Payment & Financial Data Security

Chigos does not collect, process, or store raw credit/debit card numbers, expiration dates, or 3-digit CVV security codes on its servers.

All online financial transactions are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways: Paystack (a Stripe company) and Flutterwave. When you enter payment details, you interact directly with the encrypted checkout modal provided by the gateway.

Payment Protection Guarantee

Chigos only receives an encrypted token, transaction status (success/failure), and a reference code confirming your payment. Your bank details remain strictly between you, your bank, and the licensed payment switch.

06

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser to make our website work effectively. We utilize the following classes of cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for signing in, maintaining session security, saving video player volume and progress, and routing API calls. These cannot be disabled.
  • Functional Preferences: Remembers your chosen site language, currency display (NGN or USD), and playback speed preferences.
  • Aggregated Performance & Analytics: Measures anonymous platform metrics such as page load duration and mobile streaming reliability. No personally identifiable tracking is shared.

You can manage or disable non-essential cookies at any time through our Cookie Settings page or via your browser settings.

07

Data Sharing & Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We disclose information only under the following limited conditions:

  • Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure (AWS / Cloudflare for video streaming and DDoS mitigation), transactional email providers (SendGrid/Postmark for verification emails), and payment switches (Paystack/Flutterwave).
  • Instructors: Instructors receive your display name, course enrollment date, and discussion comments in course Q&A. Instructors never receive your email address, phone number, or payment card details.
  • Legal Requirements: If compelled by a valid court order, subpoena, or law enforcement request issued by a court of competent jurisdiction in Nigeria.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred subject to the same strict privacy commitments.
08

Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active to provide continuous access to your purchased courses and verifiable certificates.

If you choose to delete your account:

  • Your name, email address, profile picture, and login credentials will be permanently erased or anonymized from production databases within 30 calendar days.
  • Financial transaction logs are retained for 7 years in accordance with Nigerian tax laws and Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) accounting mandates.
  • Issued certificate verification records remain cryptographically verifiable using their anonymous verification hash, ensuring your CV credentials remain valid.

Your Privacy Rights (NDPA, NDPR & GDPR)

Regardless of your geographical location, Chigos grants all users comprehensive data rights:

Right What It Means For You How to Exercise
Right of Access You have the right to request a complete machine-readable copy (JSON/CSV) of your personal data. Email privacy@chigoselearning.com
Right to Rectification You can update or rectify any inaccurate or incomplete personal details. Profile Settings or via Support
Right to Erasure ("To Be Forgotten") You may request total deletion of your user account and personal identifiers. Email privacy@chigoselearning.com
Right to Restrict Processing You can request that we pause processing your data in case of dispute. Contact DPO

We respond to all verified data subject requests within 72 hours, and complete fulfillment within 30 days free of charge.

10

Security Architecture & Technical Safeguards

We deploy defense-in-depth security standards to protect your data from unauthorized access, breach, alteration, or destruction:

  • Encryption in Transit: 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption across every web endpoint with strict HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) headers.
  • Encryption at Rest: AES-256 database encryption for sensitive configuration, credentials, and instructor bank records.
  • Access Controls: Role-based access controls (RBAC) and mandatory two-factor authentication (2FA) for all platform engineers and administrators.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Real-time automated vulnerability scanning, rate-limiting, and Cloudflare web application firewall (WAF) mitigation.
11

Children's Privacy Protection

Chigos is designed for learners aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where required by local legislation).

If we become aware that an account has been registered by an unsupervised minor without verified parental or legal guardian consent, we will promptly terminate the account and purge all associated personal data from our systems.
